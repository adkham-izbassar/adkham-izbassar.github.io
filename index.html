Adkham Izbassar

Petroleum Engineering · Subsurface Energy · Geologic Hydrogen

I am an M.S. student in Petroleum Engineering at Texas Tech University. My research focuses on geologic hydrogen, electromagnetic interactions with reservoir rocks, subsurface energy systems, and experimental petroleum engineering.

I am particularly interested in understanding natural and stimulated hydrogen generation in geological systems and developing technologies that can translate subsurface energy research into practical applications.

Research

Geologic Hydrogen

Investigation of natural hydrogen systems in Kazakhstan and West Texas, including hydrogen generation associated with ultramafic rocks, water-rock reactions, radiolysis, geological structures, and subsurface migration pathways.

Electromagnetic Reservoir Stimulation

Experimental investigation of dielectric properties and electromagnetic penetration depth in reservoir rocks under pressure, temperature, fluid saturation, and acoustic stimulation.

Multiphase Flow and Pipeline Systems

Experimental testing of transparent thermoplastic pipeline materials for multiphase-flow visualization, including static pressure, cyclic loading, and failure characterization.
