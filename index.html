Adkham Izbassar

I am an M.S. student in Petroleum Engineering at Texas Tech University. My research focuses on geologic hydrogen, electromagnetic reservoir stimulation, subsurface energy systems, and experimental petroleum engineering.

Research

Geologic Hydrogen

Research focused on identifying and evaluating natural hydrogen systems, including hydrogen generation associated with ultramafic rocks, water-rock interactions, radiolysis, and subsurface geological structures.

Electromagnetic Reservoir Stimulation

Experimental investigation of electromagnetic penetration depth and dielectric behavior of reservoir rocks under varying pressure, temperature, fluid saturation, and acoustic conditions.

Multiphase Flow and Pipeline Research

Experimental research involving transparent thermoplastic pipeline systems for visualization and characterization of multiphase flow.
